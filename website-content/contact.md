### SECTION 3: "NOT SURE WHAT YOU NEED?" QUICK GUIDANCE

#### H2: Unsure About the Complete Specification?
You do not need a complete laboratory analysis before speaking with us. Share whatever details you have, and our technical specialists will guide you through the selection process.

- **For Mechanical Seals:**  
  Tell us the fluid name, temperature, and shaft diameter. We will determine whether you need a balanced SF-B, an unbalanced SF-U, or an outside-mounted SF-P PTFE bellows seal.
- **For Industrial Pumps:**  
  Tell us your required flow rate (m³/hr), discharge head (meters), and fluid corrosiveness. We will recommend between metallic open-impeller, solid polymer, or polymer-lined options.
- **For Cutlass Bearings:**  
  Provide the shaft diameter and housing bore. We will supply standard metric/imperial sizes (25 mm to 400 mm) with fluted or spiral water-lubricating grooves.

### SECTION 5: FREQUENTLY ASKED TECHNICAL QUESTIONS (FAQ)

#### H2: What to Expect When Working with SealForge

**Q1: How quickly will I receive an engineering recommendation and quote?**  
*A:* For standard mechanical seals, cutlass bearings, and standard centrifugal pumps, our engineering team typically responds within 4 to 24 business hours. Complex slurry or highly corrosive lined pump duties requiring hydraulic checks are quoted within 48 hours.

**Q2: Can SealForge supply replacements for imported or obsolete mechanical seals?**  
*A:* Yes. We routinely cross-reference and manufacture drop-in replacements for standard EN 12756 seals, MG1/JC rubber bellows, and Grundfos pump seals (including BQQE, HQQE, and cartridge designs), saving clients significant downtime and import markups.

**Q3: Can cutlass bearings be manufactured to custom dimensions?**  
*A:* Absolutely. While our standard catalogue covers shafts from 25 mm to 400 mm, we manufacture custom outer diameters, custom lengths, and flanged shell arrangements to customer drawings.

**Q4: Do you provide material test certificates?**  
*A:* Yes. All engineered deliveries can be accompanied by raw material certificates, face flatness inspection reports, and hydraulic hydrostatic pressure test reports upon request.
