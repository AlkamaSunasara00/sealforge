### SECTION 3: MATERIAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

#### H2: Material Selection Matched to Real Operating Conditions
Mechanical equipment reliability begins with material compatibility. A seal or pump failure is almost never random—it is caused by incorrect material selection for the fluid, temperature, or solids content.

- **Sealing Face Pairings:**
  - **Silicon Carbide vs. Silicon Carbide:** Universal chemical resistance and extreme hardness for abrasive slurries and high pressures.
  - **Carbon Graphite vs. Silicon Carbide / Ceramic:** Low friction and self-lubricating performance for clean water, hydrocarbons, and oils.
  - **Tungsten Carbide:** Unmatched fracture toughness for severe mechanical shock and viscous sludges.
- **Secondary Elastomers:**
  - NBR (oils/water up to 120 °C), EPDM (steam/hot water/dilute acids up to 150 °C), FKM/Viton (hydrocarbons up to 200 °C), PTFE and FFKM for extreme chemical attack.
- **Pump Metallurgy & Fluoropolymer Liners:**
  - Wetted parts cast in Carbon Steel, CF8M (316 SS), Hastelloy C, or moulded in PP, GRPP, UHMWPE, PVDF, and PFA for total corrosion immunity.
- **Engineering Design Standards:**
  - Manufactured and verified in alignment with **EN 12756**, **ISO 5199 / ISO 2858**, and **ANSI B73.1 / ANSI B16.5 Class 150**.
