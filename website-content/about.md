### SECTION 2: THE INDUSTRIAL CHALLENGE & OUR MISSION

#### H2: Built for Equipment That Cannot Afford to Fail
In industrial fluid handling, a seal failure or pump breakdown is rarely just a maintenance issue—it stops entire production lines, causes catastrophic chemical spills, and damages high-capital rotating shafts.

Most industrial failures stem from a single root cause: **generic components applied to unique operating duties**. A mechanical seal chosen without accounting for chemical concentration will fail in days; an industrial pump without appropriate polymer lining will pit and crack; an unaligned bearing without proper hydrodynamic water flutes will burn out under friction.

**The SealForge Mission:**  
To deliver engineered-to-duty sealing and pumping solutions backed by uncompromising material science and direct application engineering. We ensure every seal, pump, and bearing supplied is precisely matched to the pumped fluid, operating temperature, pressure envelope, and equipment geometry.

### SECTION 3: THREE ENGINEERING DIVISIONS UNDER ONE ROOF

#### H2: Three Specialized Disciplines, One Engineering Core

#### 1. Mechanical Seals Division
- **Scope:** 7 comprehensive product families covering balanced (SF-B), unbalanced (SF-U), PTFE bellows (SF-P), heavy-duty single-coil spring (SF-S), tapered coil (SF-T), elastomer bellows (SF-MJ), and Grundfos pump interchange seals (SF-G).
- **Engineering Focus:** Face flatness within 2 light bands, hydraulic balance optimization, and advanced metallurgy (Alloy C-276, 316SS, Sintered SiC, Tungsten Carbide).

#### 2. Industrial Pumps Division
- **Scope:** Non-API overhung centrifugal pumps in metallic (Carbon Steel, 316SS, Hastelloy), solid polymer moulded (PP, GRPP, UHMWPE), armoured polymer, and polymer-lined constructions (PVDF, FEP, PFA).
- **Engineering Focus:** Robust hydraulic performance up to 600 m³/hr and 160 m head, temperatures up to 230 °C, and chemical transfer immunity against boiling acids and aggressive alkalis.

#### 3. Cutlass Bearings Division
- **Scope:** Precision water-lubricated sleeve bearings for shafts from 25 mm to 400 mm in both cylindrical sleeve and integral flanged geometries.
- **Engineering Focus:** Resilient elastomeric linings with longitudinal fluted or spiral hydrodynamic water grooves bonded into metallic (marine brass, bronze, stainless steel) or non-metallic composite shells for zero-grease marine propulsion and vertical pump support.

### SECTION 4: RIGOROUS ENGINEERING STANDARDS & MATERIAL INTEGRITY

#### H2: Engineering Standards Aligned with Global Industry
SealForge products are designed, manufactured, and evaluated against recognized international standards:

- **Mechanical Seals:** Designed in compliance with **EN 12756 (formerly DIN 24960)** dimensional standards for seamless interchangeability in standard stuffing boxes.
- **Centrifugal Pumps:** Engineered according to **ISO 5199 / ISO 2858** and **ANSI B73.1** dimensional and hydraulic criteria, with **ANSI B16.5 Class 150** flange connections.
- **Water-Lubricated Bearings:** Manufactured to tight tolerance clearances ensuring hydrodynamic fluid-film formation and continuous debris expulsion.

#### Material Integrity & Science:
- **Primary Faces:** Sintered Silicon Carbide, Reaction-Bonded Silicon Carbide, Tungsten Carbide (Nickel/Cobalt binder), Resin/Antimony Carbon Graphite, and High-Purity (99.5%) Alumina Ceramic.
- **Elastomer Compounds:** Genuine NBR, EPDM, FKM (Fluorocarbon), FFKM (Perfluoroelastomer), and pure virgin/filled PTFE.
- **Metals & Alloys:** 316 Stainless Steel, Alloy C-276 (Hastelloy equivalent), Naval Brass, Phosphor Bronze, and specialized alloys.

### SECTION 5: HOW WE WORK WITH PLANT & MARINE ENGINEERS

#### H2: An Engineering Partnership from Duty Specification to Installation
We do not operate as an impersonal parts distributor. We act as an extension of your plant engineering and maintenance team:

1. **Datasheet Review:** We begin with your exact operating datasheet—evaluating fluid concentration, temperature, pressure spikes, solids percentage, and shaft speed.
2. **Material Compatibility Check:** We analyze the complete chemical interaction across wetted faces, secondary elastomers, pump casings, and bearing linings.
3. **Dimensional Verification:** For replacement duties (including Grundfos, MG, and JC series), we verify shaft diameters, installation lengths, and housing tolerances to guarantee direct drop-in fit.
4. **Supply & Technical Backing:** Every product is supplied with verified material certificates, installation instructions, and responsive technical support.
